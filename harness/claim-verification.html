Detectability — what an outside library says about us

We publish "2 of 9 adversarial detectors fire", measured by us. CreepJS is not ours and it is built specifically to catch spoofing: it counts "lies", collects "trash" (values that are impossible rather than merely unusual), and tries to name the privacy tool. If it flags us that is a finding, not a failure — we already publish that it is detectable.
