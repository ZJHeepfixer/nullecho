      Nullecho blocks trackers and shows each site a different, internally consistent
      device profile, which breaks the fingerprint that follows you between sites.
      It does not hide your IP address or your TLS fingerprint, it changes
      nothing about what a site you are signed in to knows, and sites can tell it is
      installed. On Firefox or Brave, their built-in protections are stronger.
