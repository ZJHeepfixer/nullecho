### 🔴 E0 — THE PROOF ONLY WORKS IN A BROWSER. Read this before sending anything.

**Verified live 2026-09-20, both ways:**

| Check | In a real browser (Chrome) | Under `curl` (no JS) |
|---|---|---|
| `…StatuteText?article=gcl&section=13-321…` | **Returns the full § 13–321 statute text** | **"File not Found"** |
| `…StatuteText?article=gcl&section=13-322…` | **"File Not Found"** | "File not Found" |
| `…StatuteText?article=gcl&section=13-408…` | **Returns text; (a) reads "a violation of § 13–321 of this title"** — no "or § 13-322" | "File not Found" |

**The statute body is rendered client-side.** A bare HTTP client gets "File not Found" for *every*
section, including the positive control. So:

- ⛔ **Never tell a recipient to `curl` these URLs.** They will get "File not Found" on 13-321 too,
  conclude the control is broken, and discount the whole finding — the exact failure mode
  BRIEF-PITCH.md warns about ("without it a 'File Not Found' is equally consistent with a typo or
  an outage").
- ✅ **Always write "open it in a browser"** — every draft in this folder does. Keep it that way.
- This is the project's standing lesson applying to itself: *grep proves source, only rendering
  proves what users see.* The earlier sweep's curl-only checking is also the most likely reason a
  second agent could have "confirmed" the wrong thing.
