**Subject:** Maryland's algorithmic-pricing disclosure section was struck before passage — statute takes effect October 1

Hi,

A tip for whoever covers state privacy and pricing law.

Maryland HB 895 (Ch. 154) is described across client alerts and trade coverage as enacting an
all-merchant disclosure duty — Com. Law § 13-322, mandating "THIS PRICE WAS SET BY AN ALGORITHM OR
BY USING YOUR PERSONAL DATA" from October 1. The section was struck by amendment before passage.
Maryland enacted § 13-321 only, a food-sector ban that mandates no wording.

Checkable in a browser: the state's statute endpoint returns "File Not Found" for `section=13-322`
and statute text for `section=13-321`; codified § 13-408(a) and § 13-411(a) each read "a violation
of § 13-321 of this title" with no "or § 13-322"; the chapter print carries "and 13-322" struck.

I build an open-source privacy extension and read the statutes for a feature. Not a lawyer. I have
a sourced brief I'll send free.
