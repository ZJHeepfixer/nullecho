Hi [name],

Maryland's Protection From Predatory Pricing Act is widely summarised as enacting two things: a
food-sector dynamic-pricing ban, and a second section — Com. Law § 13-322 — requiring every
merchant that prices on personal data to display "THIS PRICE WAS SET BY AN ALGORITHM OR BY USING
YOUR PERSONAL DATA" from October 1.

That second section was struck by amendment before passage. Three checks, each about a minute in a
browser:

- `mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gcl&section=13-322&enactments=true&archived=false`
  returns "File Not Found"; the same URL with `section=13-321` returns statute text.
- Codified § 13-408(a) and § 13-411(a) each read "This section does not apply to a violation of
  § 13-321 of this title." No "or § 13-322."
- In the Ch. 154 print, the enacting clause carries "and 13-322" struck through.
