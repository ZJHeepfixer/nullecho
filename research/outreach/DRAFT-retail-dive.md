**Subject:** Retailers building a Maryland pricing banner for October 1 are building to a section that was struck

Hi,

Maryland's Protection From Predatory Pricing Act takes effect October 1, and a lot of the guidance
written for retailers describes a second section — Com. Law § 13-322 — requiring any merchant that
prices on personal data to display "THIS PRICE WAS SET BY AN ALGORITHM OR BY USING YOUR PERSONAL
DATA."

That section was struck by amendment before the bill passed. What Maryland actually enacted is
§ 13-321: a ban on dynamic pricing by food retailers over 15,000 square feet and third-party food
delivery, with no wording mandated at all.

The practical angle for your readers: a general retailer putting up a Maryland banner is complying
with nothing — and in food retail, where the conduct is banned, a banner is an admission rather
than a cure.
