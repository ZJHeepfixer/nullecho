Hi,

A tip for whoever covers state privacy and pricing law.

Maryland HB 895 (Ch. 154) is described in at least one law-firm client alert, and in the
AI answers of the major search engines, as enacting an all-merchant disclosure duty — Com.
Law § 13-322, mandating "THIS PRICE WAS SET BY AN ALGORITHM OR BY USING YOUR PERSONAL
DATA" from October 1.
The section was struck by amendment before passage. Maryland enacted § 13-321 only, a
food-sector ban that mandates no wording.

Checkable in a browser:
https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gcl&section=13-321&enactments=true&archived=false
returns the enacted § 13-321 text; swap in section=13-322 and the same endpoint returns
"File Not Found." Codified § 13-408(a) reads "a violation of § 13-321 of this title" with
no "or § 13-322", § 13-411(a) likewise names only § 13-321, and the chapter print carries
"and 13-322" struck.

I raised the same point in a public comment to the FTC (docket FTC-2026-1057). I build an
open-source privacy extension and read the statutes for a feature. Not a lawyer. I have a
sourced brief I'll send free.
